# Retirement Accounts Guide

Retirement accounts offer tax advantages to help you build wealth for the future.

## BEGINNER LEVEL
Start early – compound interest works wonders.  
- $500/month at 8% from age 25 = ~$1.7 million by 65; starting at 35 only yields ~$745,000.  

**401(k)**: Employer‑sponsored; pre‑tax contributions, often with a match that is free money.  
**Traditional IRA vs Roth IRA**:  
- Traditional: pre‑tax now, taxed later.  
- Roth: after‑tax now, tax‑free withdrawals.  

## INTERMEDIATE LEVEL
Contribution limits (2024):  
- 401(k): $23,000 (or $30,500 for 50+).  
- IRA: $7,000 (or $8,000 for 50+).  

**Backdoor Roth IRA**: Contribute to Traditional IRA (non‑deductible), then convert to Roth – bypasses income limits.  
**Mega backdoor Roth**: After‑tax 401(k) contributions converted to Roth.  
**RMDs** (Required Minimum Distributions) apply to Traditional accounts at age 73; Roth IRAs have no RMDs during your lifetime.

## ADVANCED LEVEL
- **Asset allocation** by age: gradually reduce equities as retirement nears.  
- **Sequence of returns risk**: Early retirement market crashes are most damaging. Mitigate with 2–3 years of expenses in cash/bonds.  
- **Social Security** timing: delay to age 70 for higher lifetime benefits.  
- **HSAs** as a retirement tool: triple tax advantage (deductible contributions, tax‑free growth, tax‑free withdrawals for medical expenses). After 65, can be used for any purpose (taxed as income, no penalty).
