Forex (Foreign Exchange) Basics Guide

What is Forex?
The Foreign Exchange market (Forex or FX) is the global marketplace for trading currencies. It's the largest and most liquid financial market in the world, with daily trading volume exceeding $7 trillion.

Key Features:
- 24-hour market (5 days/week)
- Highly liquid
- Low transaction costs
- High leverage available
- Decentralized (no central exchange)
- Accessible to retail traders

Currency Pairs:
- EUR/USD (Euro/US Dollar)
- USD/JPY (Dollar/Yen)
- GBP/USD (Pound/Dollar)
- USD/CHF (Dollar/Swiss Franc)
- AUD/USD (Aussie/Dollar)
- USD/CAD (Dollar/Canadian)
- NZD/USD (Kiwi/Dollar)

Pip:
- Smallest price movement
- Usually 4th decimal (0.0001)
- For JPY pairs, 2nd decimal (0.01)
- EUR/USD 1.1050 to 1.1051 = 1 pip move

Lot Size:
- Standard Lot: 100,000 units
- Mini Lot: 10,000 units
- Micro Lot: 1,000 units
- Nano Lot: 100 units

Getting Started:
1. Learn basics thoroughly
2. Practice on demo account
3. Start with major pairs
4. Use low leverage initially
5. Focus on risk management
6. Keep detailed trading journal
7. Start with small real money positions"""
