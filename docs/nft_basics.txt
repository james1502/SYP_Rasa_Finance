NFT (Non-Fungible Token) Basics Guide

What are NFTs?
Non-Fungible Tokens (NFTs) are unique digital assets stored on a blockchain. Unlike cryptocurrencies (which are fungible - one Bitcoin equals another Bitcoin), each NFT is distinct and cannot be exchanged 1:1 with another NFT.

Key Characteristics:
- Uniqueness: Each NFT has unique identifying information
- Ownership: Blockchain proves who owns the NFT
- Transferability: Can be bought, sold, or transferred
- Programmable: Smart contracts can add functionality
- Interoperability: Can work across different platforms

How NFTs Work:
1. Creator mints (creates) NFT on blockchain
2. NFT contains metadata (name, description, media link)
3. Ownership recorded on blockchain ledger
4. Can be listed for sale on marketplaces
5. Smart contracts handle transfers and royalties

NFT Use Cases:

Digital Art:
- Digital paintings, illustrations, generative art
- Artists can sell directly to collectors
- Royalties on secondary sales (typically 5-10%)
- Examples: Beeple, Art Blocks, XCOPY

Collectibles:
- Profile pictures (PFPs): Bored Ape Yacht Club, CryptoPunks
- Trading cards: NBA Top Shot, Sorare
- Virtual items with community membership benefits

Gaming:
- In-game items (skins, weapons, characters)
- Play-to-earn mechanics
- Cross-game asset portability (potential)
- Examples: Axie Infinity, Gods Unchained

Music & Entertainment:
- Music NFTs with royalty sharing
- Concert tickets as NFTs
- Exclusive content access
- Examples: Sound.xyz, Royal

Virtual Real Estate:
- Land in metaverse platforms
- Examples: Decentraland, The Sandbox, Otherside

Domain Names:
- Blockchain domains as NFTs
- Examples: ENS (.eth), Unstoppable Domains

Major NFT Marketplaces:

Ethereum:
- OpenSea: Largest NFT marketplace
- Blur: Trader-focused, aggregator
- Foundation: Curated art platform
- SuperRare: High-end digital art

Solana:
- Magic Eden: Largest Solana marketplace
- Tensor: Professional trading platform

Bitcoin:
- Ordinals: NFTs on Bitcoin (inscriptions)
- Magic Eden (Bitcoin): Ordinals marketplace

NFT Standards:
- ERC-721: Original NFT standard on Ethereum
- ERC-1155: Multi-token standard (fungible + non-fungible)
- SPL: Solana NFT standard
- BRC-20/Ordinals: Bitcoin NFT standard

Minting NFTs:
1. Choose blockchain (Ethereum, Solana, etc.)
2. Create digital content
3. Select marketplace or minting platform
4. Upload content and set metadata
5. Pay gas fees to mint
6. List for sale (fixed price or auction)

Risks and Considerations:
- Market Volatility: NFT values can drop significantly
- Liquidity Risk: May be hard to sell
- Copyright Issues: Minting doesn't transfer IP rights
- Scams: Fake collections, phishing, rug pulls
- Environmental Concerns: Some blockchains use significant energy
- Storage: Media often stored off-chain (IPFS, Arweave)

Evaluating NFTs:
- Creator reputation and track record
- Community strength and engagement
- Utility beyond speculation
- Rarity and supply
- Historical trading volume
- Roadmap and future plans

Tips for Beginners:
1. Research before buying
2. Start with small amounts
3. Verify collection authenticity
4. Use reputable marketplaces
5. Secure your wallet
6. Understand gas fees
7. Don't invest more than you can afford to lose
