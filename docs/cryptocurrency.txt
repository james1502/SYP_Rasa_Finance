# Cryptocurrency Fundamentals

Cryptocurrencies are digital currencies secured by cryptography and operating on decentralized networks.

## BEGINNER LEVEL
Bitcoin is the first and largest cryptocurrency. It has a capped supply of 21 million coins.
Ethereum powers smart contracts and decentralized applications.
To buy crypto, sign up on an exchange like Coinbase or Kraken, verify your identity, link your bank account, and make a purchase.
Hot wallets are connected to the internet and convenient. Cold wallets are hardware devices and most secure.

## INTERMEDIATE LEVEL
Blockchain is a distributed and immutable ledger.
Cryptocurrency is extremely volatile. Bitcoin has seen gains over 1000 percent and crashes of 70 percent or more.
Only invest 1 to 5 percent of your portfolio in crypto.
Use dollar cost averaging to reduce risk.
Stablecoins like USDC and USDT are pegged to the US dollar.
DeFi offers lending, borrowing, and yield farming without traditional banks.

## ADVANCED LEVEL
Proof of Work is used by Bitcoin. Proof of Stake is used by Ethereum 2.0 and uses 99 percent less energy.
Layer 2 solutions include Lightning Network for Bitcoin and Polygon for Ethereum. They provide faster transactions and lower fees.
Smart contract vulnerabilities have led to billions of dollars in hacks.
Tokenomics includes circulating supply, total supply, market cap, and fully diluted valuation.
Every crypto trade is a taxable event. Short term gains are taxed as ordinary income. Long term gains are taxed at capital gains rates.
